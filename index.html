01 · Method development

State-Adaptive Proportional Policy Smoothing (SAPPS)

A trajectory-local policy-regularization method for continuous-control reinforcement learning that scales the penalty on consecutive action changes according to changes in observed state.

Implemented with PPO and evaluated across MuJoCo continuous-control benchmarks, simulated wavefront-sensorless adaptive optics, and real Crazyflie altitude-control experiments through multi-environment, multi-seed studies.

  • Policy Regularization
  • PPO
  • PyTorch
  • MuJoCo

02 · Research software

Adaptive Optics Gym

A configurable Gymnasium-compatible environment for reinforcement-learning control of wavefront-sensorless adaptive optics in optical satellite communication downlinks.

Models atmospheric turbulence, optical propagation, deformable-mirror control, focal-plane sensing, and single-mode-fiber coupling, with PPO, SAC, and DDPG workflows.

03 · Sim-to-real robotics

Crazyflie Reinforcement Learning

An end-to-end simulation-to-hardware PPO workflow for Crazyflie 2.1 altitude control.

Combined multi-seed simulation training with 100 real-hardware episodes, real-time telemetry, safety constraints, and automatic landing.

04 · Physics simulation

MuJoCo Truck-Packing Simulator & PPO Agent

A completed Gymnasium-compatible MuJoCo research prototype for sequential 3D truck packing.

Includes contact physics, boundary and overlap checks, displacement-based stability detection, three occupancy-grid projections, continuous placement actions, and PPO training with custom encoders.

05 · Learning from demonstration

Robotic Deburring Skill Learning

Dynamic Movement Primitive representations of human deburring skills learned from 6-DOF and 1-DOF haptic-device demonstrations.

Included particle swarm optimization for DMP identification, adaptable trajectories, force-trajectory methods, and evaluation on a hexapod platform.
